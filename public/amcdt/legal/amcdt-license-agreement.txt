1. ABOUT THE SOFTWARE

1.1 What AMCDT does. AMCDT is a diagnostic software tool. It compares output
traces from a model with output traces from corresponding code (for example,
in CSV or MAT file format) and explains observed differences in plain
language. Typical explanations include rounding, saturation, library mismatch,
compiler effects, or a possible real bug.

1.2 What AMCDT is not. AMCDT is an engineering aid only. Its explanations are
suggestions to help your engineers investigate differences; they are not
conclusions, approvals, or sign-offs. In particular:
(a) AMCDT is not a functional-safety tool and is not designed, tested, or
    intended to establish that any model, code, system, or product is correct
    or safe.
(b) Mercy Systems makes no claim that AMCDT complies with, or is qualified,
    certified, or validated under, ISO 26262, IEC 61508, or any other
    functional-safety, quality, or regulatory standard, and no tool
    qualification or certification evidence is provided.
(c) AMCDT is not a substitute for your own verification, validation, testing,
    review, safety processes, and qualified engineering judgment, all of which
    remain your responsibility.

1.3 Business use only. AMCDT is offered only to businesses and professionals
for business purposes. You represent that you are acquiring the Software in a
business or professional capacity and not as a consumer for personal, family,
or household purposes.
