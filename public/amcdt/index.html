Stop hunting for mismatches

When model output and code output disagree, finding the first bad signal and the reason behind it can take days of side-by-side plotting. AMCDT does the searching for you: it shows which signal is off, the exact point where the difference starts, and the most likely cause, written in plain language your whole team can act on.

How it works

  1. Load your traces

    Open the output traces from your model and from your generated or hand-written code. CSV and MAT files are supported.

  2. Run

    AMCDT lines the traces up and checks every signal, sample by sample, for differences.

  3. Read the answer and export

    Each mismatch is listed with the signal, where it starts, and what is causing it, in plain language. Export the result as an HTML or JSON report.

What you get

  • Every mismatch pinpointedWhich signal differs and the exact sample and time where the difference starts.
  • The likely cause, namedRounding, saturation, library mismatch, compiler effect, or a real bug that needs a closer look.
  • Plain-language explanationsClear findings that engineers, leads, and customers can all read.
  • HTML and JSON reportsShare a readable report, or feed the JSON into your own tools and records.
  • Your data stays with youAnalysis runs locally on your machine. Trace files are never uploaded; only the license check goes online.
  • Built for small teamsSimple licensing for suppliers, Tier 2 / Tier 3 companies, and engineering consultancies: one license per engineer’s machine, monthly, yearly, or one-time.
