Enter your license key

  1. Start AMCDT from your applications menu (or run amcdt in a terminal).
  2. On first start, AMCDT asks for a license key. Paste the key from your email or from the purchase page and confirm.
  3. AMCDT activates the key for this machine. Each key works on one machine; buy one license per machine.
