Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This notice explains what MERCY Systems (Danny Hebel, 398 Log Road, Spring City, Tennessee 37381) collects when you use the Any Video to 4K website, subscribe, or use the Android or Windows apps. Questions: danny@mercysystems.com.

The short version

  • Your videos are converted on your own phone or computer. Video files are not uploaded to us.
  • Stripe handles payment. Danny never sees your full card number.
  • Your unlock key is emailed from licenses@mercysystems.com (via Brevo).
  • The apps contact Keygen only to check and activate your key, using an anonymous device fingerprint plus a short device label.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Checkout and subscription. When you subscribe ($4.99/month for UNLIMITED 4K videos), Stripe collects your payment details on its secure checkout. We receive from Stripe information needed to fulfill and support your subscription — typically your email address, payment status, and Stripe customer/subscription references. Card numbers stay with Stripe; Danny never sees or stores your full card number. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

Unlock key email. After you subscribe, we email your unlock key from licenses@mercysystems.com using Brevo (email delivery). That email uses the address from checkout. See Brevo’s privacy policy.

Key activation (the apps). To unlock Unlimited conversions, the Android or Windows app validates your key with Keygen (keygen.sh). Each check or activation sends:

  • your unlock key;
  • an anonymous device fingerprint — a one-way hash of a device ID (the raw device ID never leaves your device);
  • the device platform (Android or Windows);
  • a short device name for the license dashboard (for example Windows 10 PC, or your phone’s make and model).

Keygen (and normal internet routing) may also see technical connection data such as IP address and time. The apps do not send your videos, file names, or conversion results.

Website. These /4k pages do not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts of their own. Hosting and network providers may keep ordinary server logs (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) to deliver and protect the site.

Support email. If you email danny@mercysystems.com, we use what you send to answer and keep related records.

Your videos stay on your device

Conversion runs on your phone or computer. The apps do not upload your videos to MERCY Systems or to Keygen. Only the unlock-key checks described above leave the device for licensing.

How we use information

We use this information to take payment, email your unlock key, activate and check your subscription on up to one phone and one computer, send important notices about your subscription, provide support, prevent abuse, and keep business records.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who help run the product: Stripe (payments), Brevo (email), and Keygen (license checks). We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Retention

Subscription, payment, and license records are kept as long as needed to provide the service, handle cancellations or disputes, and meet legal or tax requirements. You can cancel anytime through the Stripe customer portal; access lasts through the end of the paid period, then the key stops working. Server logs are kept only as long as useful for security and operations.

Your choices

Manage or cancel your subscription via the link on the Any Video to 4K page (Stripe customer portal). For access, correction, or deletion requests about information we hold, email danny@mercysystems.com.
