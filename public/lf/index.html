Lunar Frontier

A real-time strategy game set on the Moon. Command your forces across six regions as a rival nation expands from the same starting ground.

The game

War for the Moon

Lunar Frontier is built with Godot Engine. Choose Campaign to wage a war against a rival nation, or First Flag to practice with no enemies. Then pick Easy, Medium, or Hard — the difficulty changes how quickly the rival expands — and start in Tranquility with your mobile command vehicle and troops.

Deploy command centers, build a base, and command units across six lunar regions, each with its own look:

  • Tranquility
  • Crater Rim
  • Ember Reach
  • Frost Pole
  • Silver Basin
  • Dark Side

In Campaign mode, both sides begin in Tranquility. The rival then expands in a fixed order through the remaining regions. You win only after the rival has settled all six regions and every rival building and unit on the Moon is destroyed.
