Automotive Model-Code Diagnostic ToolAMCDT

Stop hunting for mismatches. Find the cause and fix it.

AMCDT pinpoints exactly where your model and code outputs differ and what is causing it, so your team stops spending hours searching side-by-side plots and can fix the problem right away.

  • Every mismatch pinpointed. See which signal differs and the exact sample and time where the difference starts.
  • The likely cause, named. Rounding, saturation, library mismatch, compiler effect, or a real bug that needs a closer look, explained in plain language with HTML and JSON reports.
  • Your data stays with you. Analysis runs locally on your machine. Trace files are never uploaded; only the license check goes online.

02 / About MERCY Systems

Precision technology.
Practical purpose.

MERCY Systems is an American technology company building precision technology across software and hardware.

We are starting where engineers lose the most time: our first product, AMCDT, is diagnostic software that shows engineering teams where model and code outputs differ and why. Hardware, including electronic control systems, is in development.

Our mission is to deliver precise technological capabilities that combine exceptional performance, reliability, and commercial affordability. We believe technological innovation should do more than demonstrate what is possible. It should provide practical solutions that businesses can afford, integrate, and deploy.

Our commercial philosophy

Performance is part of the picture.

We focus on more than performance specifications. We consider the complete commercial picture: cost, efficiency, integration requirements, reliability, and long-term scalability.

Our objective is straightforward: make precision capabilities economically practical without unnecessary complexity or expense.
