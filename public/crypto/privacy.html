Privacy notice

Effective November 8, 2026 (America/Chicago).

Who operates this service

Crypto Rebound Scanner is a Mercy Systems market-research publication. This notice covers its Android and Windows apps, published-results server, and product page.

Device trial and subscription records

The app hashes an operating-system identifier with its app identity before sending it over HTTPS. Android uses its app-scoped Android ID; Windows uses MachineGuid. The raw identifier and your personal computer name are not sent. The server applies an additional keyed hash and stores the resulting device reference, claimed device platform, first-use time, and subscription-device allocation. This is used to keep the 14-day trial from restarting after ordinary reinstalls and to limit one subscription to one Android device and one Windows device.

The first app launch starts the device trial without a payment card or email address. Deleting local files does not delete the server's trial record. Factory resets, OS reinstalls, identifier changes, and modified clients can weaken this protection.

Payments and unlock-key delivery

If you subscribe, Stripe collects payment and billing information on its hosted checkout. Mercy Systems does not receive your full card number. We keep the Stripe subscription/customer references, access state, billing-period end, and unlock-key verification hash. Your checkout email is used to send your key through Brevo. Queued key-delivery content is encrypted at rest. Protect the key as you would a password.

Technical information

Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and connection information to deliver and secure the service. The API uses short-lived access tokens. We do not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics in these apps or the product page. The Android app requests network access, not contacts, location, photos, or raw hardware identifiers.

Sharing and retention

DigitalOcean hosts the scanner; Stripe processes billing; Brevo delivers subscription email; CoinMarketCap provides public market data and does not need your device identity. These providers may process information under their own terms. Records are kept only as reasonably needed for access control, billing, security, dispute handling, legal obligations, and backups. Minimal pseudonymous anti-abuse records may be retained separately when legally permitted. Operational retention periods must be finalized before launch.

For access, correction, deletion, or device replacement requests, use Mercy Systems contact details. We will review requests under applicable law and explain any required retention.
