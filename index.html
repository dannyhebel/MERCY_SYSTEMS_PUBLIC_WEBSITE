How It Works

Three simple steps.

1

You Define

We agree on one representative control problem, the baseline to compare against, the operating boundaries, and the performance metrics that matter.

2

We Process & Verify

MERCY is evaluated side-by-side against the agreed baseline under the defined test conditions. We measure tracking error, boundary violations, stability, and runtime.

3

You Receive

You receive a concise technical result package showing the measured comparison and the next-step recommendation:

  • Baseline vs. MERCY metrics
  • Constraint / violation results
  • Runtime and tracking results

If MERCY shows a meaningful advantage, the next step can be an extended pilot, integration discussion, or commercial license.

Current Production Scope

Precisely defined service boundaries.

MERCY Systems is currently focused on deterministic closed-loop control evaluation for robotics and autonomous systems. The first commercial step is a controlled technical evaluation rather than a broad production deployment.

  • Robotics and autonomous-system control problems

  • Measured tracking error, stability, violations, and runtime
  • Private technical evaluations with a fixed, agreed scope
  • Approved, non-sensitive and unrestricted evaluation inputs only

What this service is

A controlled evaluation of MERCY control technology against a defined baseline on a representative problem.

The goal is straightforward: establish measurable evidence before asking a customer to consider integration.

Current benchmark evidence: in the tested MERCY V4 vs. Büchi V3 closed-loop benchmark, MERCY recorded 1.79×10⁻⁸ final error vs. 8.95×10⁻², 0 vs. 21 velocity violations, 63.5% vs. 20.4% time inside ±0.05, and 0.00515 s vs. 0.00672 s closed-loop runtime. These results were established through MERCY Systems' eight-month comparative control research program.
