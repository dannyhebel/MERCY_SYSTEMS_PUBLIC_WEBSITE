02 / ABOUT MERCY SYSTEMS

Precision technology.
Practical purpose.

MERCY Systems is an American technology company focused on precision hardware, high-performance computing, and next-generation electronic control systems.

Our mission is to deliver precise technological capabilities that combine exceptional performance, reliability, and commercial affordability.

We believe technological innovation should do more than demonstrate what is possible. It should provide practical solutions that businesses can afford, integrate, and deploy at scale.

Our work spans control, computing, intelligent electronic systems, and precision engineering, with applications across aerospace, transportation, robotics, industrial automation, and other demanding environments.

Our commercial philosophy

Performance is part of the picture.

At MERCY Systems, we focus on more than performance specifications. We consider the complete commercial picture: production costs, system efficiency, integration requirements, reliability, and long-term scalability.

Our objective is straightforward: make precision capabilities economically practical without unnecessary complexity or expense.
