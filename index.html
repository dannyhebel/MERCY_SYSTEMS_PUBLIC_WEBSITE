Extreme-Condition Drone Controller

MERCY Storm™

Designed for demanding flight environments where wind, turbulence, payload shifts, and other disturbances can challenge conventional control systems. MERCY Storm is built to deliver smooth everyday flight while continuously supporting stronger corrective response as conditions become more demanding.

  • Always-on stability: no separate extreme-condition mode required.
  • Disturbance-resistant control: designed to counter unwanted aircraft movement.
  • Normal-flight performance: smooth operation when conditions are calm.
  • Mission-focused hardware: aimed at search and rescue, inspection, public safety, and demanding field operations.

GNSS-Independent Drone Controller

MERCY Blackout™

Designed for operations where satellite positioning may become degraded, obstructed, or unavailable. MERCY Blackout is intended to support onboard navigation and controlled flight without continuous dependence on GNSS.

  • GNSS-independent operation: designed to support controlled navigation through positioning interruptions.
  • Onboard navigation: keeps critical navigation capability with the aircraft.
  • Signal-loss resilience: built for environments where external positioning cannot always be trusted.
  • Recovery-oriented design: supports defined navigation and recovery behavior when positioning is interrupted.
